Privacy policy

This policy describes Lazy Fields: Idle Farm, the Android game with package name com.dataframegamestudio.lazyfields, published by Data Frame Game Studio. It was last updated on 21 October 2026. You can read it without signing in.

Farm save on the phone

The game stores your farm on the device. That save includes progress such as gold, gems, crops, animals, cats, settings, and a record of purchases granted in the game. Uninstalling the app can remove this copy.

Play Games sign-in and cloud save

If you sign in with Google Play Games, the app requests that account so it can upload and download your farm save through Play Games Saved Games. Google provides the app with your Play Games player id and display name. The uploaded save is the same farm progress stored on the phone. Sign-in is optional. If you stay signed out, the farm remains only on that device.

You can disconnect the app from the Google account that approved it. Cloud save then stops until you sign in again.

Purchases

Optional purchases, including VIP and gem packs, are handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment. The game stores that the purchase was granted so it can restore VIP and avoid granting the same purchase twice.

Ads

The game may show ads through Google Mobile Ads, including an optional rewarded ad. Those services may process device and advertising information under Google's own policies. VIP removes ads.

In-game mail

Letters shown in the game are loaded through Firebase. That request is used to deliver mail content, not to build a marketing profile.
